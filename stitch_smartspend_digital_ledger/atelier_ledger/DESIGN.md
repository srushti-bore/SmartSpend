## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an airy, unhurried, and intellectually satisfying personal finance environment. It replaces the anxiety-inducing clutter and heavy, synthetic green tints of conventional fintech apps with the serene cadence of classical editorial publishing and modern architectural restraint. 

### Design Movement: Uncrowded Editorial Modernism
The aesthetic marries the generous negative space of luxury art monographs with the structured clarity of bespoke accounting journals:
- **Serenity & Breathing Space:** Density is treated as a design defect. Generous padding (`p-6`, `p-8`) and wide margins create a quiet sanctuary for long-term financial contemplation.
- **Strict Anti-Green Tinting:** Heavy mint/emerald/forest floods are eliminated. Surfaces are grounded in warm eggshell and pale porcelain, letting data breathe without artificial chromatic noise.
- **Hairline Architectural Cadence:** Visual groupings are established through crisp, single-pixel hairline borders and quiet tonal shifts rather than floating cards, box shadows, or glossy containers.
- **Typographic Poise:** High-contrast editorial figures meet an austere humanist grotesque, treating personal net worth and transaction ledgers as works of lasting personal record.

## Colors

The color system delivers an airy, calm light mode environment inspired by artisanal warm rag paper, mineral pigments, and archival ink. Heavy green cast surfaces and saturated neon treatments are strictly barred.

### Foundation
- **Canvas (`#FBFBF9`):** A soft, warm porcelain white that provides an organic reading ground with zero glare.
- **Tonal Well / Surface Chalk (`#F4F3EF`):** Subtle warm chalk tone for pinned summaries, secondary tiers, and calm container backgrounds.
- **Hairline Divider (`#E2E0D8`):** A razor-thin structural line tone for row divisions, metric shelves, and perimeter rules.
- **Primary Ink (`#1E232A`):** Deep, crisp muted slate/ink text delivering authoritative contrast without synthetic black harshness.
- **Muted Ink (`#686E78`):** Restrained neutral for metadata, timestamps, and column labels.

### Pastel Accents & Semantic Signals
Accents are deployed with disciplined scarcity, functioning as quiet typographic annotations rather than wide surface fills:
- **Periwinkle / Slate Blue (`#5B7598` / `#E8EDF5`):** Used for investment categories, transfer states, active segment markers, and navigational anchors.
- **Pastel Amber / Honey (`#E2A746` / `#F6E6C9`):** Highlights attention points, goal milestones, pending clears, and primary focal highlights.
- **Pastel Coral / Rose (`#D66853` / `#FCE8E4`):** Signals outflow, debits, warnings, and budget thresholds.
- **Pastel Sage / Mint (`#5E8B76` / `#E6EFEA`):** Used strictly and sparingly for positive cash inflows, completed reconciliations, and surplus balances.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere where numeric values and written statements carry equal gravity. 

- **Editorial Serif (`Newsreader`):** Applied to core financial indicators, grand totals, net worth hero statements, and journal section titles. It provides humanist warmth, subtle calligraphic flare, and high-legibility proportional or tabular numerals.
- **Architectural Grotesque (`IBM Plex Sans`):** Delivers clean utilitarian structure for descriptions, audit timestamps, action labels, and numerical breakdowns.
- **Numeric Alignment & Tabular Lining:** All tabular transaction amounts utilize proportional lining figures formatted strictly with fixed vertical baseline alignment.
- **Label Craft:** Small and medium labels are styled in small uppercase with expansive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), reflecting classical ledger column heads.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is defined by open horizons, rhythmic vertical spacing, and deliberate negative space that prevents informational cognitive overload.

### Grid & Form Factor Reflow
- **Mobile (Base):** Fluid single-column layout with generous outer margins of `1.5rem` (24px) preventing edge crowding. Content sections stack sequentially, isolated by large vertical rhythm gaps (`space-xl`).
- **Tablet (768px+):** 6-column fluid structure with `2rem` gutters. Metric summaries dock on the left two columns; chronological transaction lines flow through the right four columns.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column architecture constrained within a centered `1120px` maximum bounding width, framed by expansive `4rem` outer canvas padding to preserve optimal scanning lines.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Anti-Box Paradigm:** Components are rarely enclosed in padded, isolated boxes. Instead, horizontal hairline rules partition vertical spaces.
- **Generous Padded Zones:** Structural sections leverage uncrowded internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`), ensuring figures never press against boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids drop shadows, blurred ambient lighting, and glossy glassmorphism. Visual depth is established solely through planar tonal differentiation and delicate hairline boundaries.

### 1. Hairline Boundaries
- **Primary Rules:** Clean 1px solid hairlines (`#E2E0D8`) define horizontal registers, row divisions, and column groupings.
- **Ledger Finish Rules:** Final balance summaries and month-close summaries are accented with double hairline rules (two 1px lines separated by 3px of canvas space).
- **Transient Boundaries:** Bottom sheets and drop-in panels use a single top hairline rule without under-surface shadow halos.

### 2. Tonal Surface Strata
- **Base Ground (Level 0):** Eggshell canvas (`#FBFBF9`) spanning the entirety of the primary viewports.
- **Recessed Well (Level 1):** Flat warm chalk panels (`#F4F3EF`) with 1px hairline perimeter borders, used sparingly for pinned metrics, sticky header bands, or filter summaries.
- **Floating Panels (Level 2):** Flyout drawers and modal dialogues remain flat porcelain (`#FBFBF9`), bounded by an ink hairline rule and subtle semi-transparent chalk scrim (`#1E232A` at 12% opacity).

## Shapes

The shape language reflects architectural precision and fine paper stationery:

- **Soft Micro-Radii (`roundedness: 1`):** A restrained `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius applied to inputs, interactive chips, and interactive action buttons. This softens corners while maintaining structural rectilinear alignment.
- **Full Bleed & Zero-Radius Rows:** Transaction rows, section breaks, and data tables connect with zero radius directly across horizontal boundaries.
- **Pill Badges:** Small status indicators and audit chips utilize full pill borders (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical stamps from structural form elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid slate/ink fill (`#1E232A`), crisp warm eggshell text (`#FBFBF9`), `0.25rem` border radius, no shadow. Hover or press transitions subtly reduce opacity to 90%.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background bounded by a 1px hairline border (`#E2E0D8`), slate text (`#1E232A`). Hover transitions introduce a warm chalk wash (`#F4F3EF`).
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Borderless, subtle slate ink text with an underline style for quiet editorial dismissals or secondary routes.

### Cards & Metric Shelves (The Uncarded Concept)
- Floating card containers are explicitly omitted.
- Metrics sit directly on the canvas as "Metric Shelves," bounded above and below by 1px hairline rules (`#E2E0D8`) with generous padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- High-level net worth values are set in monumental `Newsreader` (`display-lg`), paired with an understated `label-sm` heading in tracked uppercase.

### Transaction Rows
- Set full-width, separated strictly by horizontal 1px hairline dividers.
- **Leading Area:** Payee or merchant name in `IBM Plex Sans` (`title-md`), seated above an understated category tag and date in `body-sm`.
- **Trailing Area:** Monetary balance rendered in `Newsreader` (`headline-sm`). Inflow metrics feature an upward glyph (`↑`) and soft pastel sage ink (`#5E8B76`); outflows feature a soft coral hue (`#D66853`).

### Input Fields
- Underlined ledger register style or full-width flat warm chalk fields (`#F4F3EF`) with a 1px hairline border (`#E2E0D8`) and `0.25rem` radius.
- Labels float cleanly above inputs in tracked uppercase (`label-sm`).
- Focus states transition the bottom hairline border to crisp ink slate (`#1E232A`) at 1.5px thickness without glowing shadows.

### Chips & Filters
- Understated rectangular tags with soft `0.25rem` radius or pill badges.
- Inactive state uses transparent ground and 1px hairline border (`#E2E0D8`) with ink slate text.
- Active state fills with pastel periwinkle subtle (`#E8EDF5`) and slate blue text (`#5B7598`), bounded by a matching tone-on-tone 1px stroke.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp rectilinear checkboxes (`0.125rem` radius) and circular radio buttons outlined in 1.5px muted ink slate.
- Checked state fills with slate ink (`#1E232A`) and presents a sharp white check glyph without glowing outer halos.
